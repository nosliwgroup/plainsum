Questions

Where are my pages saved?

In your browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere, which is also why results show up instantly.

Do I need to learn any code?

No. You type the way you’d write a note. Type /help on any line to see every page type, command, and abbreviation.

Where do the calories come from?

From USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy, 2018), which includes some fast-food items. For anything missing, type the calories once, like shake shack burger 530 cal, and Plainsum remembers it.

Are currency conversions live?

No. Plainsum uses fixed, approximate rates and marks those answers with ≈. Set your own with a line like 1 eur = 1.10 usd.

Is there a desktop app?

Not yet. Plainsum runs in your browser for now.
